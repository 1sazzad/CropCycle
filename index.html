The idea

From satellite signals to a practical rotation.

CropCycle combines environmental context with farmer priorities, then ranks candidate crops using a transparent resilience score.

01

Describe the farm

Choose location, current crop, soil type, season, irrigation access, and the farmer's main goal.

02

Read NASA signals

The prototype maps farm conditions to rainfall, temperature, soil-moisture, and vegetation indicators inspired by NASA datasets.

03

Score crop options

Each crop receives suitability scores for water demand, climate tolerance, soil benefit, and rotation compatibility.

04

Explain the choice

Farmers see not only a recommendation, but also the reasons, trade-offs, and alternatives.
